Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Instagram Discord Bot ("the Bot") stores and how that information is used.

1. Information Stored

The Bot may store: Instagram usernames selected for monitoring; Discord server, channel, and role information required to send notifications; and Instagram post identifiers used to prevent duplicate notifications.

2. How Information Is Used

This information is used only to operate the Bot's Instagram notification functionality, including determining which Discord role should be mentioned when a monitored Instagram account publishes a new post.

3. Personal Information

The Bot does not intentionally collect or sell users' private personal information. It does not require Discord users to provide passwords, payment information, or private Instagram credentials.

4. Data Sharing

The Bot does not intentionally sell or share stored configuration information with third parties. It may communicate with third-party services such as Discord and publicly available Instagram/RSS infrastructure as necessary to provide its functionality.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Configuration and notification records are retained while needed for the Bot to operate. The Bot owner can remove monitored accounts and delete the Bot's local database to remove stored records.

6. Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect the Bot's configuration and credentials. Users should never share a Discord bot token or other credentials publicly.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Bot's owner through the Discord server where the Bot is installed.
